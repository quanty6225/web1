As you all know, you can subscribe TickTick tasks into your Calendar application. To further narrow the boundary between calendar & TickTick, now, you can subscribe calendar events into TickTick web interface.  At this moment, this feature is exclusive to Pro users.  First, enable ‘Calendar Subscription’ in 'Labs’ Then, in 'list view’ section of the left panel, you will see the 'Calendar Subscription’ folder with a ’+’ icon Click on the ’+’ icon and add the Calendar URL in the pop-up dialog window In addition to the web interface, you can subscribe calendar into TickTick Android app as well. 

Group your tasks by using tags on TickTick

We believe that you’ve seen tags everywhere and definitely, most of you love it. Therefore, we’ve released this feature (though in beta right now) and now let’s see how you can use it effectively on TickTick.  The ‘Tags’ list is hidden by default. So, you should first show it by editing the lists.  Then, you can start creating tasks with tags. It is quite simple. Just add the # symbol in front of any word or phrase.    When clicking on the highlighted content, a 'tags’ icon will appear in right bottom corner and when click on it, you will be directed to the task list labeled by this specific tag.  Now, you can attach at most 5 tags to each task And you can see all tags by clicking the 'Tags’ list.
