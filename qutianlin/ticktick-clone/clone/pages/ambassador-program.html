Spread the power of productivity as a TickTick Ambassador

As the to do lists and task organizer loved by people across the world, TickTick is rewarding Ambassadors who are spreading the power of productivity together with us.

If you have your own brand, are passionate to recommend TickTick to your audience, and are ready to earn rewards proportional to the amount of Premium purchase, then you are more than welcome to apply for the TickTick Ambassador Program from here.

More details for being a TickTick Ambassador:

  • Referral Link:
    • If your application successfully goes through the review process, we will generate a referral link specialized for you.
    • The link will be associated with the TickTick account email provided by you in the application form.
  • Valid Period for Referral:
    • An invitation relationship is established when an invitee registers for a TickTick account via your referral link.
    • Any amount a certain invitee pays for TickTick Premium within the first year of creating an account will earn you rewards.
  • Rewards Rates:
    • Upgrade to Premium from the Web, via PayPal or Stripe, the reward rate is 30%.
    • Upgrade to Premium from Google Play Store or App Store, the reward rate is 15%.
  • Payouts:
    • Payment method: PayPal.
    • Payout date: 15th of each month.
    • Minimum payout per time: $25. Awaiting payouts under $25 will be accumulated to be paid together until the rewards reaches the minimum payment amount.
  • Dashboard:
    • There will also be a Dashboard for you to keep track of your invitation and rewards anytime you want. The Dashboard can be accessed from ticktick.com/ambassador_dashboard once your referral link is generated. After logging in by then, you'll be able to check the Dashboard.
  • Special Perks:
    • TickTick also offers perks specialized for your audience or network. We will write to you with more details on this in response to your application.

Thank you in advance for willing to join us in spreading the power of productivity! We will reach out within 5 work days if you are eligible to become a TickTick Ambassador.
