Get Familiar with Three Calendar Related Functions in TickTick

A few days ago, a user asked me how to view her calendar events in TickTick . I sent her detailed instruction and took it into granted that she could make it by herself. However, she was still confused and kept asking me over the next few days. Her persistence impressed me a lot and I started thinking maybe many of you also have this similar confusion. Considering that Google Calendar is the most widely used calendar, I will take it as an example to show you how you can get most out of TickTick with these calendar related functions. Display TickTick tasks in Google Calendar 1. Sign in TickTick via a Web browser. 2. Click the avatar on top right corner to enter “Settings” > “Calendar Subscription”.  3. Click “Enable” and then, you will get a URL. Yes, copy it! 4. Sign in your Google Calendar and click the downward arrow of “Other calendars" to choose “Add by URL". 5. Paste TickTick’s URL into the pop-up window. So easy, right? 6. Then you will find your TickTick tasks displayed in Google Calendar. That’s it! Show Google Calendar events in TickTick This feature is available to TickTick Pro users only.

How to request a refund for Android App?

If you’re dissatisfied with TickTick, you can apply for a refund within 14 days after purchase. Paid with Google Wallet:1. Open the Google Play Store app and sign in;2. Touch the Menu icon > My Apps > Subscriptions or search TickTick directly in Play Store; 3. Select TickTick to view its detail page > Cancel your monthly/Yearly subcription; 4. Tap on YES to confirm your choice. Paid with Paypal:Send your Paypal Account and Transaction ID at support@ticktick.com and your refund request will be processed within 3 business days.
