Help translate TickTick into all possible languages

TickTick is using an online translation platform Weblate to handle our localization efforts and multilingual support. The team is sincerely grateful for our users who do the work voluntarily.

If you:

  • cannot find your language supported in TickTick, and
  • are willing to help make TickTick available in all possible languages

Please fill in the form here. After you click submit, you’ll get the link to sign up Weblate. There will also be a link to the translation guide to help you set up and get started on Weblate.

We thank you for all your support and contribution.
