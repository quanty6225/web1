Get your finger ready to swipe tasks!

TickTick’s Android weekly release will never let you down. In this week’s version, we provided two features, which will further enable you to action quickly & easily to realize productivity enhancement.  First: Get your finger ready to swipe tasks! You can slide right or left to choose different menus for quick actions.  It’s not difficult at all. We have designated four different colors for frequently used actions: complete & archive a task, set priority, set due date and move to list. For example, swipe a task to the right until the red menu appears, then, you can set priority directly.  Of course, the easiest way to understand this feature is try. Try now by yourself.  Second, set default value for new task. With this default setting enabled, you don’t have to click again & again to set values such as due date & priority. Your every newly added task will be given the default values automatically.  Slide to the right from the margin to enter list page and click ‘gear’ icon to enter Settings>New Task Defaults. For example, you can choose Today as default due date, set priority as 'High’ and get reminded 5 mins earlier than real reminder time. 

Voice input task in TickTick with 'Set Alarm'

When an idea suddenly jumps into your mind while you are driving, the best way to note down the to-do is definitely ‘voice input’. You all know that TickTick supports Google Now’s 'Note to self’ feature for 'voice input’ but it lacks the reminder function and you have to open the app to find out the task and manually set a reminder time. Now, with Google Now’s 'Set Alarm’ feature, you can voice input a task together with the reminder time.  Now, let’s show you how to make this happen: 1. Open Google Voice Search and tap the microphone  2. Start with 'Set Alarm’+reminder time+task content such as 'Set Alarm 8 pm buy milk’ 3. After the content being fully recognized by Google Now, a pop-up will appear, asking you to add this to-do in TickTick for once or always 4. Well, the task is created in TickTick with reminder time

Repeat your tasks upon completion date

Repeating task is indispensable for a good to-do list. In most cases, a task will repeat upon due date such as a weekly yoga class, a monthly paper review. But it is also not rare that some to-do’s need to be repeated upon completion. For example, Jenny has her nail to be polished every two weeks. But during her vacation outside the country, there maybe a delay and she only want this event to be recurred again upon completion date. Now, with TickTick, you have more flexible scheduling options. You can set recurring tasks to repeat either upon due date or completion date.  1. Choose ‘N days/weeks/months’ in repeating settings  2. Select 'Repeat from completion date'  3. Press 'OK’ to save your setting. 

Say hello to 'reminder in advance'

Have you ever feel frustrated when the reminder pops up while you have no enough time to get prepared for that task? Have you ever missed a deadline helplessly because you were unfortunately occupied in a con-call then?  Now, it’s time to say no to ‘due time reminder only’. TickTick’s brand new feature 'reminder in advance’ allows you to set a reminder earlier than due time so that you can better arrange your agenda or schedule.  In this version, you can choose to be reminded 5 mins, 30 mins or 1 hour before.  Click 'Custom’ and set the due time. Then, click the clock icon again to choose when to be reminded.
