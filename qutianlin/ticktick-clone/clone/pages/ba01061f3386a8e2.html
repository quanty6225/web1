Frequently Asked Questions

If my friend is already a TickTick user, can I still refer him/her?
The Referral Program is only valid for new users who haven't registered TickTick.
When can I get the Premium rewards?
Within 1 year of the successful referral, when your friend first subscribes to Premium, you will get 1 month free TickTick Premium (there'll be an instant notification in the app).
What if my friend generates a refund for his/her Premium subscription?
If your friend generates a refund after upgrading, your reward will be cancelled accordingly.
How many rewards can I earn?
There is no upper limit on rewards as long as the Referral Program is valid.
How to reach out if I have further questions about this Program?
Any questions? Please contact support@ticktick.com for more information.
TickTick reserves the right to modify, terminate or cancel the Program at any time for any reason.
