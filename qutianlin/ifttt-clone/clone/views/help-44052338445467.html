How to tell if a service requires a Pro or Pro+ plan to use

Background

When creating an Applet, you might see a message saying that it uses features that require a Pro or Pro+ subscription. This usually means the Applet includes a Trigger, Query, or Action that isn’t available on the Free tier.

Most services on IFTTT offer Triggers and Actions you can use on the free plan. However, some services include Triggers and Actions that require a Pro or Pro+ subscription to access. Queries are only available on the Pro+ tier.

This guide explains how to check whether a service or a specific Trigger or Action requires a subscription, and where to look for that information when you’re creating an Applet.

From the Service Page

You can confirm whether a service’s Triggers and Actions require a subscription by visiting its service page on IFTTT. A service page be accessed by typing the name of the service into the search bar in ifttt.com/explore then pressing the relevant service card.

Once on the service page, scroll down to the section that lists the service’s Triggers, Queries, and Actions. Each item will show whether it is available on the Free, Pro, or Pro+ tier.

From the Applet Composer

You can also see which features require a subscription while creating or editing an Applet.

After selecting a service in the Applet composer, you’ll see the list of available Triggers, Queries, and Actions. Each item will display a badge if it requires a Pro or Pro+ subscription.

Here’s how to read the badges:

Identifying Published Applets That Require a Subscription

Some published Applets available on the Explore page include Triggers, Queries, or Actions or features that require a Pro or Pro+ subscription to enable.

For more information about published Applets that require a subscription and how to identify them, see this guide: What is a Pro/Pro+ Applet?
