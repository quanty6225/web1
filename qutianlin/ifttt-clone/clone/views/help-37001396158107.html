How to get started with IFTTT

What is IFTTT?

IFTTT is a powerful automation platform that helps you connect your favorite apps, devices, and services to work together seamlessly. Whether you want to automate social media posts, control smart home devices or streamline productivity tasks, IFTTT makes it easy to create custom automations. No coding is required.

What can I do with IFTTT?

With IFTTT, you can connect over 1,000 different apps and devices, including Twitter, Dropbox, Evernote, Fitbit, and Google Assistant, to automate tasks and streamline your digital life. On IFTTT, we call these integrations services. By linking services together, you can create Applets. These automations help your apps and devices work in new and powerful ways. Whether it's cross posting across different social media platforms, controlling smart home devices, or receiving important alerts, IFTTT makes automation simple and accessible.
