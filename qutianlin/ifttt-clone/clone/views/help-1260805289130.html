Connecting to a service on IFTTT

You can connect to a service on IFTTT in many ways:

Through the explore page:

  • Visit the explore page and enter the service name in the search bar
  • Click or tap on the card for the service you searched for
  • Click or tap Connect on the service page
  • You will be redirected to the service for authorization
  • Enter your username and password, or use credentials from an OAuth service if supported (e.g. Sign in with Google)
  • Once authorization has been complete, you will be returned to IFTTT

Through the services page:

  • Visit the services page and search for your service
  • Click or tap Connect on the service page
  • You will be redirected to the service for authorization
  • Enter your username and password, or use credentials from an OAuth service if supported (e.g. Sign in with Google)
  • Once authorization has been complete, you will be returned to IFTTT

By connecting to a premade Applet if the service is not already connected:

  • Visit the services page and enter the service name in the search bar
  • Select a premade Applet that connects to the service
  • Click or tap Connect on the Applet page
  • You will be redirected to the service for authorization
  • Enter your username and password, or use credentials from an OAuth service if supported (e.g. Sign in with Google)
  • Once authorization has been complete, you will be returned to IFTTT

By creating an Applet if the service is not already connected:

  • Choose a trigger, action, or query from the service while creating an Applet in the Applet composer
  • You will be redirected to the service for authorization
  • Enter your username and password, or use credentials from an OAuth service if supported (e.g. Sign in with Google)
  • Once authorization has been complete, you will be returned to IFTTT
