How do I turn off or archive an Applet?

Turning Applets off:

  1. Visit ifttt.com or the IFTTT app
  2. Select the Applet and press the connected button to disconnect the Applet

Archiving Applets:

If you created your Applet from the Applet composer, you can archive it to remove it from My Applets. Archived Applets will no longer count towards your Applet limit if you're on the Pro or free plan.

  1. Select the Applet
  2. Scroll to the bottom to select Archive

Restoring Applets:

  1. Locate the Applet in your archive
  2. Click or tap on the Applet
  3. The Applet can then be re-enabled in the My Applets tab by pressing the Connect button

Deleting Enabled Applets:

If you'd like to remove a Published Applet that you enabled rather than created on the Applet composer, you'll have the option to delete it instead of archiving it:

  1. Select the Applet
  2. Scroll to the bottom to select Delete

Enabled Applets that have been deleted will no longer count towards your Applet limit if you're on the Pro or free plan.

Deleting DIY Applets:

For now, this is not possible on ifttt.com, but you can permanently delete Applets at https://ifttt.com/p/username/applets/private. More detailed instructions can be found here.
