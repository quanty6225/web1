Discord Applet Troubleshooting

If your activity feed indicates Missing Permissions / Missing Access for your Discord Applets, or if you're encountering Discord action failure messages, it may be because your server hasn't granted IFTTT the necessary permissions to run your Applet. 

Granting Permissions

First, go to your Server Permissions settings: Right click on your server icon > click Server Settings > Roles > select IFTTT > open Permissions.

Additional Troubleshooting Steps

If your channel has been converted to a private channel, the IFTTT bot may be removed from the server's member list, which can prevent Applets from functioning properly. To address this, click the gear icon to edit your channel settings if it is private:

Then use the add members or roles button to add IFTTT as a member of the channel:
