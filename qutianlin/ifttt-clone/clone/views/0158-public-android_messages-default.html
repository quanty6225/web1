Automate your Android SMS integrations

Android SMS and IFTTT let you trigger automations when you get texts and send messages to phone numbers. Android SMS is a native service that allows you to receive Short Message Service (SMS) messages on your device and send messages to other phone numbers. Standard carrier rates may apply. This service requires the IFTTT app for Android.
