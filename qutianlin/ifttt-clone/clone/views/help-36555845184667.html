Why Did IFTTT Create My Account with a @privaterelay.appleid.com Email?

Apple’s Private Relay (Hide My Email) is a privacy feature that lets you create randomly generated email addresses when signing into an app or website. These addresses forward emails to the email linked to your Apple ID, helping keep your real email private. You can read more about Hide My Email on Apple’s help center.

Using Hide My Email When creating an IFTTT Account

When creating an IFTTT account, if you select Continue with Apple, it will create an IFTTT account using your Apple ID. As part of the signup process, you'll see an option to enable Hide My Email.

Continue with Apple shown as an option when creating an IFTTT Account on the IFTTT iOS App

If you don’t select Hide My Email, your IFTTT account will be created using the email address linked to your Apple ID.

If you do select Hide My Email, Apple will generate a private relay address to receive emails from IFTTT related to your account.

These private relay addresses use the domain @privaterelay.appleid.com and include a randomly generated string before the @ sign. This is the email address associated with your IFTTT account.

Accessing Your IFTTT Account Created Using Hide My Email

If you created your account using Hide My Email, just go to ifttt.com/login, select Continue with Apple, and sign in with your iCloud credentials if prompted.

Tap Continue with Apple to sign in using Apple Single Sign On

How to Find Your Private Relay Address for IFTTT

If you used Sign in with Apple when creating your IFTTT account, and you're having trouble finding the email address linked to your account, here’s how you can locate it:

On iPhone:
1. Open Settings and tap your name to access Apple ID settings.
2. Tap iCloud
3. Scroll down and under the iCloud+ Features heading tap Hide my Email
4. Find and tap the IFTTT from the list.
5. The private relay email address Apple generated for IFTTT will be displayed in the Hide My Email field.

On Mac:
1. Open System Settings, then go to Apple ID.
2. Navigate to Password & Security > Apps Using Apple ID.
3. Click IFTTT, then view the address listed under Hide My Email.

On Apple’s Website:
1. Go to https://appleid.apple.com and sign in.
2. Under Sign-In & Security, click Sign in with Apple.
3. Find IFTTT, then view the email address used.

 

Can You Change the Login Email Linked to Your IFTTT Account?

Yes! If you’d prefer to use your real email instead of a private relay address, you can update your login email at any time by visiting this page in your account settings.

 

To do so, enter a new email address in the Email field then scroll down and press update. 

Can You Use a Private Relay Address to Send Emails on IFTTT?

At the moment, Apple’s Private Relay addresses don’t allow sending emails through IFTTT’s Gmail or Email services. If you're using either service, you'll need to update the connected email to a regular address via the service connection page in your IFTTT account.

 

Seeing Duplicate Charges on IFTTT?

If you’re seeing duplicate charges, it's possible you have more than one IFTTT account; one with your real email and another using your Private Relay address. Try logging in using the Continue with Apple option to check for a second account tied to a private relay email.
