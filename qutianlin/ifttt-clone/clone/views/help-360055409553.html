Can I subscribe to IFTTT Pro or Pro+ with PayPal?

Yes. While PayPal is not currently supported as a direct payment method for IFTTT subscriptions, you can add PayPal as a payment method to Apple Pay or Google Pay and use it when subscribing to IFTTT Pro or Pro+.

Add PayPal to Apple Payments on iOS

To link PayPal to Apple Pay:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Select Payment & Shipping.
  4. Tap Add Payment Method.
  5. Select PayPal.
  6. Sign in to PayPal and follow the on-screen instructions.

Once PayPal is linked, select Apple Pay when subscribing through the IFTTT mobile app.

Add PayPal to Google Pay

To link PayPal to Google Pay:

  1. Open Google Pay and add PayPal as a payment method.
  2. Sign in to PayPal and follow the on-screen instructions.

Once PayPal is linked, select Google Pay when subscribing through the IFTTT mobile app.

You can also use Apple Pay or Google Pay when subscribing on IFTTT.com with a linked PayPal account.
