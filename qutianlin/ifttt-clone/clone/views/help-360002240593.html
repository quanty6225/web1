Why is my Email Applet not working?

The Email service is not compatible with every domain. However, if you use an email address with a common domain such as Gmail.com, iCloud.com, or Outlook.com, your Applets should work as expected.

By default, the email address matches your account email.

To connect a different email address to IFTTT, head to the Email service settings page and click Reconnect.
