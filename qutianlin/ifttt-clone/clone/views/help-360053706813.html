IFTTT Plans at a glance

For users, IFTTT comes in three flavors - Free, Pro and Pro+. Visit the Plans page for pricing and a full comparison of all IFTTT plans.

Free

On the Free plan, you can enable a limited number of Applets. Either create them yourself from the Applet composer or choose from the millions of published Applets that have already been made from the ifttt.com/explore.

Applets on the free tier can include one Trigger and one Action. You can also add a delay before the Action in your Applets.

The Free plan has no subscription fees.

Pro

The Pro plan has extra perks.

  • You can create and enable up to 20 Applets
  • Multiple Actions can be included in a single Applet
  • Pro users' Applets run faster
  • Increased rate limits for certain services
  • Applets can include Triggers and Actions from Pro services such as Twitter and Webhooks

Pro+

Pro+ unlocks our best and most powerful tools.

  • Create and enable unlimited Applets
  • Add logic to your Applets with Queries and filter code
  • Connect multiple accounts per service
  • Access to the IFTTT AI Services

Do you offer a free trial?

When you sign up for Pro or Pro+, you'll receive a 7-day free trial automatically, as long as you haven’t already had a trial.

Do you support annual billing?

Yes! You can subscribe to any paid plan on a monthly or yearly basis.
