Signing in to IFTTT with Google, Facebook, or Apple

IFTTT offers a couple of different sign-in methods, called Social login, in addition to the standard email and password login. Current options include signing in with Apple, Google, and Facebook.

Can I still log in using my username and password?

Absolutely. New users signing in with Social login for the first time still have a password, it's just randomly generated. The only time you may need to use it is if you're connecting IFTTT to a service that doesn't play well with Social login. You can update your password at any time by resetting it.

Can I still use two-step verification with Social login?

Of course! If you have it set up, you’ll be prompted to enter your two-step verification code when you sign in.

I already have an IFTTT account. Can I use Social login?

You sure can, just make sure the email on your IFTTT account matches the one on your Google, Facebook, or Apple account. Otherwise, you may have an extra IFTTT account on your hands. If everything looks good, go to your account settings and scroll to Linked accounts to set up Social login.

Why do I still have to connect Google, Facebook, and Apple separately?

Using Google, Facebook, or Apple to sign in to your IFTTT account doesn't give IFTTT the same types of permissions needed for other services to function. You'll still need to connect those individually.

Can I remove a Social login from my account?

Yes, to do so go to https://ifttt.com/settings, scroll down to the Linked accounts heading, then click or tap unlink.
