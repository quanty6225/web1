Can I subscribe annually/yearly to IFTTT Pro and Pro+?

Yes! To subscribe annually to IFTTT Pro or Pro+ as a new user, please follow these steps:

  1. Sign into your account. If you are new to IFTTT, create an account
  2. Go to ifttt.com/plans
  3. Select the appropriate plan, Pro or Pro+
  4. Select the payment option Billed yearly
  5. Complete your payment details
  6. Click Complete purchase

If your subscription was purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, you can switch to annual billing through the subscription management settings for the respective store.

To switch an existing subscription between monthly and annual billing, go to ifttt.com/billing and select Switch to monthly or Switch to annual under your plan details.
