SMS Content Guidelines for IFTTT Applets

When using the IFTTT SMS service, the Send me an SMS action lets you send a message directly to your phone. This is useful for staying notified about important events, such as upcoming meetings, motion detected by cameras, new leads, and more.

To ensure messages conform to carrier guidelines, any SMS sent through IFTTT Applets must follow our content guidelines. Applets that generate messages with prohibited content may be disconnected or removed.

Restricted SMS Content

Messages should not include content that:

  • Relates to illegal activities in your geographic location. This includes, but is not limited to:

    • Cannabis: Messages about cannabis products, even if terms, images, or links are not explicitly included.

    • CBD: Messages about CBD products where prohibited.

    • Prescription Medication: References to prescription medication that cannot legally be sold over-the-counter.

  • Contains hate speech, harassment, exploitative or abusive language, or originates from hate groups.

  • Is fraudulent or intended to deceive.

  • Contains malicious content, such as malware, viruses, or harmful links.

Following these guidelines helps ensure your Applets continue working reliably.
