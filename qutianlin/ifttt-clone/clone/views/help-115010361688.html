How do I manage or add new widgets on my device?

Any Applet that uses the Button, Note, or Camera services can be a widget. Here's how to add and manage them.

iOS

View your Widgets in the IFTTT app

Applets that use one of the services above will appear in the Widgets section under your profile in the IFTTT app. You can access your profile by tapping the profile icon in the bottom-right corner of the IFTTT app, then tapping Widgets.

Add an IFTTT Widget to your iOS device

You can add IFTTT Widgets to your Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Today View on iOS.

For instructions on adding and customizing Widgets, see Apple's guide on how to add and edit Widgets on your iPhone.

How to add widgets on Android

Adding widgets from the IFTTT app on Android:

  1. Open the IFTTT app and tap on your widget Applet
  2. Next to 'You may use this widget on your home screen.', tap Add
    • Note: The instructions you receive for adding the widget to your home screen are handled by the Launcher app, not IFTTT, so you may receive different instructions than below

How to add widgets from the home screen on Android:

  1. Long press any empty space on your home screen and tap Widgets
  2. Scroll down to find IFTTT, then long-press either IFTTT Large or IFTTT Small and drag onto an empty space on your home screen
  3. Select your Applet
