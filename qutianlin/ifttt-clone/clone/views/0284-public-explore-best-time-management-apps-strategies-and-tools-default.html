Understanding time management

Time management is about using your time strategically. We all have the same number of hours in a day, yet what separates the most productive among us is how they spend these hours.

The ability to accomplish important tasks, automate manual work, and delegate when appropriate is a unique skill that is hard to master. For many, it's difficult to get started changing habits related to effective time management, so it can be helpful to use a digital tool.

Above all it is important to make conscious decisions about how you spend your time and understand the most valuable tasks in your day. In this article, we'll cover strategies and tools that can give you the boost you need in your productivity journey.

Key benefits of effective time management:

  • - Reduced stress levels and better mental well-being
  • - Increased productivity and goal achievement
  • - Enhanced decision-making capabilities
  • - Better work quality through focused attention
  • - Improved professional reputation
  • - More free time for personal activities

Common time management challenges include digital distractions, poor planning, task overload, and more. Emails, social media updates, and alerts from different apps can pile up quickly and become a constant interrupter in your day. Moreover, meetings with unclear agendas or a never-ending to-do list can make it feel like you not being productive on a daily basis.

Time management tools can help address these challenges through smart scheduling, customized notification, automated task prioritization, and intelligent reminders. These tools can support your work patterns and create optimal times for different activities to help you maintain focus and productivity throughout your day.

Other notable time management apps

IFTTT's automation capabilities shine even brighter when paired with dedicated time management apps. Here are three powerful tools to enhance your productivity stack:

Todoist: your smart task manager

Todoist offers AI-powered task organization that adapts to your habits, along with intuitive natural language input for creating tasks. The app features priority levels with color-coding to help visualize task importance, plus collaborative features for team projects and shared lists. One standout feature is its location-based reminder from IFTTT's mobile app, which can prompt you about tasks when you're in relevant locations.

RescueTime: automatic time tracking

RescueTime is a comprehensive time tracking solution that streamlines productivity monitoring and optimization. The app automatically monitors time spent across applications and websites, delivering detailed reports on digital productivity patterns while categorizing activities based on their productivity level. Users can enhance their focus by blocking distracting websites during dedicated work sessions, and stay informed through daily and weekly productivity reports.

Motion: AI-powered scheduling

Motion takes a different approach to time management: instead of you deciding when to do each task, its AI automatically slots your to-dos into open spots on your calendar based on priority and deadline, then re-schedules them as your day shifts. For anyone whose time management problem is less "I don't have a list" and more "I don't know when I'll actually get to it," that auto-scheduling is the main draw.

These apps complement IFTTT by providing specialized features for task management and time tracking. You can create powerful workflows by connecting them through IFTTT, check out some of the examples below.

Top time management strategies

Setting clear goals transforms your time management apps from simple task lists into powerful productivity engines. Let's explore proven strategies to maximize these digital tools:

Goal setting frameworks

Some useful goal-setting frameworks include creating SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) as your foundation for success. You can break large projects into smaller, manageable tasks to maintain momentum and prevent overwhelm. Set deadlines for each milestone to keep yourself accountable and on track. IFTTT can help you to automate progress tracking and reminders, allowing you to stay focused on achieving your objectives.

Effective task prioritization

There are dozens of techniques that can improve your task prioritization, including creating an urgent-important matrix, setting up custom notifications around high-priority items, and schedule automated task reviews.

These can help you understand the items of your task list that are the most important, the most impactful, and will help you feel accomplished.

Utilize time blocks

Time blocking transforms your calendar into a powerful productivity tool. This method involves dedicating specific time slots to particular tasks, creating a structured schedule that eliminates decision fatigue and reduces context switching.
