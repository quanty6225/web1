    Frequently Asked Questions
    What is an Apple Account?
    An Apple Account is the personal account you use to access Apple services like the App Store, iCloud, Messages, the Apple Online Store, FaceTime, and more. It includes the information you use to sign in, as well as all the contact, payment, and security details that you’ll use across Apple services.
    What is the difference between an Apple ID and an Apple Account?
    With the releases of iOS 18, iPadOS 18, macOS Sequoia, and watchOS 11, Apple ID is renamed to Apple Account for a consistent sign-in experience across Apple services and devices, and relies on a user’s existing credentials.
    When do I use my Apple Account?
    Any time you set up a new device, make a purchase, or use any Apple service, you will be asked to sign in with your Apple Account email or phone number and password. Once signed in you’ll have access to the service and all your personal information.
    How many Apple Accounts do I need?
    Just one. Use the same Apple Account everywhere you sign in to ensure that all your Apple services and devices work together seamlessly and you can access your personal information from all your devices.
    If I have multiple Apple Accounts can I merge them together?
    Apple Accounts are for individual use and cannot be merged together.
    Can I share an Apple Account with someone else?
    Your Apple Account should not be shared with anyone else. It provides access to your personal information including contacts, photos, device backups, and more. Sharing your Apple Account with someone else means you are giving them access to all your personal content and may lead to confusion over who actually owns the account. To share purchases, photos, a calendar, and more with someone else, try Family Sharing Opens in a new window., iCloud Photo Sharing Opens in a new window., or other easy-to-use sharing features.
    How can I keep my Apple Account secure?
    Security and privacy are very important to Apple and we provide a number of ways to secure your Apple Account and protect your privacy including strong passwords, two-factor authentication, and more. Learn more about security and your Apple Account Opens in a new window..
    Do I have to pay for an Apple Account?
    No, you do not need to pay for an Apple Account. Learn more about how to create an Apple Account Opens in a new window..
