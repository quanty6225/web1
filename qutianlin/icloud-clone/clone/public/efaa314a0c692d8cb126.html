    iCloud+

    It’s peace of mind. Plus.

    Get started

    Find your fit.

    iCloud automatically keeps all your photos, files, and more backed up and available anywhere you go.1 And with iCloud+, you’ll get additional storage and enhanced privacy features, and you can even share your plan with up to five other family members at no extra cost.2

    To see how much storage you’re currently using, go to
    Settings [your name] iCloud on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

    Everything
    that matters.
    Saved and sound.

    iCloud Photos. Keep a lifetime of high-res photos and videos securely stored in iCloud — with space-saving versions on all your devices. So whether it’s a video of your daughter’s first steps or a portrait from her college graduation, every memory is accessible and shareable no matter where you are.3

    Shared Albums. Easily combine your and your friends’ road trip photos, or keep your family updated on the kids’ latest adventures. Everyone can add their own photos, videos, and comments over time, anytime — including people on Android or Windows devices.3

    Shared Photo Library. Share the photos and videos you want with up to five other people. Shared library content appears in Featured Photos and Memories so you can relive your adventures using photos everyone took.

    iCloud Drive. The files and folders you save are easy to get to and share, no matter which device you’re using. Access them all from the Files app on iOS and iPadOS, the Finder on your Mac, File Explorer for Windows PCs, or iCloud.com.

    iCloud Backup. The important data on your iPhone and iPad, including all your favorite apps, is automatically backed up whenever your device is connected to power and Wi-Fi — so your backup is always up to date.1

    Restore. Seamlessly bring all your photos, files, contacts, apps, settings, and more with you, so when you’re setting up a new device, it will feel as familiar as your old one.

    Your everyday essentials, available everywhere.

    iCloud.com. Easily view, manage, edit, and share what you have stored in iCloud from any browser, on any device. And with the iCloud for Windows app, you can access photos and files you’ve saved to iCloud directly from your PC.

    Visit iCloud.com

    Notes. Jot down a quick to-do list on your Mac and open it on your iPhone while you’re out and about.

    Calendar. Keep track of your schedule on the go and share events like book club meet-ups and spring break plans with friends and family.

    Safari. Your tabs and bookmarks stay up to date automatically, so you can switch devices and pick up your online shopping or school research project right where you left off.

    Passwords and Keychain. Securely store your passwords and use AutoFill to make signing in to your favorite apps and websites simple.

    Contacts. Phone numbers and email addresses of your friends and family are kept in sync, so it’s easy to reach out to anyone from any device.

    Collaboration. Share files, folders, documents, and more by sending a link. Control who can view, edit, or share the work. And with live updates, you’ll see edits as they’re being made.

    More privacy.
    More protection.
    More peace of mind.

    iCloud Private Relay. Take your privacy to the next level when browsing with Safari. Prevent websites and network providers from building a profile about you based on your IP address, location, and browsing history. Just turn it on and you’re instantly protected.4

    Hide My Email. Generate unique, random email addresses that automatically forward to your personal inbox, so you don’t have to share your real email address when filling out a form on the web or signing up for a newsletter. You can even reply without sharing anything you don’t want to.

    HomeKit Secure Video. Any activity detected by your security cameras will be recorded, analyzed on your home hub device, and uploaded to iCloud so you can view it in the Home app from anywhere.5 Footage is end-to-end encrypted, so you can trust that only the people you choose can access it.

    See compatible security cameras

    Stay connected.
    From small stuff
    to big events.

    Apple Invites.6 Create unique invitations for your biggest moments. Pick a background or use Apple Intelligence to generate an image and design your invitation. Use Apple Music, Photos, Maps, and more to bring your event to life. Then send it to all your guests and track their RSVPs. It’s everything you need to get the party started, and it’s included in your iCloud+ subscription.

    Learn more

    iCloud Mail. Seamless integration with the Mail app makes it easy to send and receive email from anywhere. Security features like spam protection and antiphishing are built in to detect and block junk mail before it reaches your inbox. It’s simple to set up your own @icloud.com email address, and when you subscribe to iCloud+, you’ll be able to personalize your email with a custom domain.

    Messages. When you sync Messages in iCloud, your conversations always go smoothly since every message is backed up, end‑to‑end encrypted, and kept in sync across all your devices.

    Questions? Answers.

    • iCloud+ is a premium subscription that provides additional storage to keep your photos, videos, files, and more backed up and automatically available across your devices. It also includes access to event creation in Apple Invites, iCloud Private Relay, Hide My Email, Custom Email Domain, and HomeKit Secure Video.

    • It’s easy. Just click one of the “Get started” links on this page, or go to iCloud Settings on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to upgrade to iCloud+.

    • Yes. You can share any iCloud+ plan with up to five other people in your Family Sharing group at no additional cost. Sharing iCloud storage with your family requires a quick additional setup — and each person’s files, documents, and other personal information remain private and separate. Learn how to share iCloud storage with your family.

    • Yes. The first time you sign in to an iPhone, iPad, or Mac with your Apple Account, you’ll get 5GB of free iCloud storage. This allows you to back up your data and keep your personal information in sync across your devices and at iCloud.com. To get additional storage for all your data, as well as access to iCloud Private Relay, Hide My Email, HomeKit Secure Video, and more, simply upgrade to iCloud+.

    • Yes. iCloud+ is included as part of all Apple One plans, so you can get all of Apple’s services with a single subscription. You can also combine an iCloud+ subscription with Apple One for even more total storage.
