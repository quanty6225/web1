Frequently asked questions

  • How does your Idea Screening solution work?

    Idea Screening from SurveyMonkey LaunchPad uses sequential monadic testing methodology to evaluate up to 30 ideas. Each respondent sees one idea at a time, helping reduce comparison bias and capture clearer feedback. The Idea Screening solution then automatically analyzes results with scorecards, statistical significance testing, benchmarks, Key Driver Analysis, and AI-powered insights—so you can quickly see which idea has the strongest market potential and why.

  • How much does the Idea Screening solution cost?

    You only pay for the research you run—no subscription, no long-term commitment. 

    Your cost comes down to two things:

    1. A fixed solution fee: This covers the methodology, guided setup, and your automated analysis. Survey your own contacts at no additional charge. The solution fee is $1,000.

    2. Targeted responses (optional): If you want, you can get idea screening feedback from your target market using our global panel. Price varies based on the number of responses and targeting—get an estimated cost. Learn more about our market research panel.

  • What can you test with the Idea Screening solution?
    • Rough sketches and storyboards
    • Mood boards and style scapes
    • Conceptual wireframes
    • Early ad and campaign ideas
  • How long does it take to get results?

    If you choose to send your survey to our integrated panel, you can often go from launch to a full analysis in hours. It really depends on your audience sample, but even for niche global audiences, most studies wrap up within 24–48 hours.

  • How many ideas can I test at once?

    You can test up to 30 ideas in text, video, or image format.

  • How do you ensure the responses are high quality?

    Our powerful data quality controls ensure reliable respondent data at scale—so you know your results are accurate.

    • Recruitment of proprietary, trusted partners
    • AI-powered bot and fraud detection
    • Regular panel calibration studies and updates to panel and respondent profiles ensure quality responses
    • Double opt-in system, panelist satisfaction monitoring, and limited survey invitations to reduce professional survey taking
    • Data cleaning for accurate and actionable results
  • What is the difference between monadic testing and sequential monadic testing?

    Monadic testing has respondents evaluate one concept at a time, mimicking how a consumer would encounter a product on the shelf or in an ad. This eliminates comparison bias. Results are compiled into scorecards alongside industry benchmarks to highlight the winners and predict in-market performance.

    Sequential monadic testing has respondents evaluate a series of concepts in a randomized order. By asking the same follow-up questions for each idea, you can easily compare results to find the winner. This method requires fewer total respondents, delivering the insights you need faster and at a lower cost.

    For example, let’s say you needed to vet 10 different campaign ideas, with a goal of narrowing it down to the top three concepts. With a monadic study, you’d need 200 responses for each of the 10 concepts, for a total of 2,000 responses. With a sequential monadic study, each respondent would see a subset of four ads, so you would only need a sample size of 500 to get 200 responses per ad. That’s a big saving in terms of panel costs, and a much faster study to field.

  • What is the difference between the Idea Screening and Product Concept Testing solutions?

    Product Concept Testing uses a monadic testing methodology where each respondent only evaluates a single concept. Idea Screening uses a sequential monadic testing methodology where each respondent evaluates multiple concepts in sequence.

    Choose Product Concept Testing when you:

    • Have 10 or fewer product concepts, and they’re well-baked
    • Need more in-depth feedback (more attributes, more open-ended feedback)
    • Want more rigorous results (no order bias, reduced survey fatigue)
    • Have broader audience targeting that allows for a larger sample size

    Choose Idea Screening when you:

    • Need to narrow down more than 10 product concepts
    • Are earlier in the product development process (rough sketches and conceptual wireframes)
    • Are okay with directional results given respondents will respond to multiple concepts which introduces order bias
    • Have more niche targeting where only a smaller sample size would be feasible
