Frequently asked questions

  • What is SurveyMonkey LaunchPad?

    SurveyMonkey LaunchPad is a suite of market research solutions equipped with expert methodologies that handle everything from survey logic to analysis, enabling you to quickly validate concepts and launch standout products and campaigns.

  • How much does SurveyMonkey LaunchPad cost?

    With SurveyMonkey LaunchPad, you only pay for the research you run—no subscription, no long-term commitment. 

    Your cost comes down to two things:

    • A fixed a-la-carte price per solution (e.g., Product Concept Testing), covering the methodology, guided setup, and your automated analysis. Pay as you go—one study at a time. Each solution has its own price so check out the specific page for details.
    • (Optional) Panel responses. If you want, you can get feedback from your target market using our global panel. The price for responses varies based on how many you need and how specific your targeting is—get an estimated cost. Learn more about our market research panel.
  • What is the SurveyMonkey global panel?

    Our global, on-demand market research panel allows you to tap into feedback from an audience of 335M+ people in over 130 countries, with over 200 targeting options.

    Learn more about our market research panel or get an estimated cost.

  • Can I target any audience with SurveyMonkey LaunchPad?

    Yes, you can target a specific audience with SurveyMonkey LaunchPad. LaunchPad integrates with our proprietary panel of 335M+ people in 130+ countries, so you can get access to millions of respondents across the globe.

    Learn more about our market research panel or get an estimated cost.

  • How fast can I get results?

    You can get results in hours by using SurveyMonkey LaunchPad and our market research panel together. SurveyMonkey LaunchPad allows you to deploy complex studies in minutes and our integrated panel allows you to collect high-quality feedback from your exact niche audience. Automated AI-powered analysis helps you understand your data, plus, with custom reporting and exports, you can quickly share presentation-ready insights.
