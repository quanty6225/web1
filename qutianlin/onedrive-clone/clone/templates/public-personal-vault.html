An extra layer of security with OneDrive Personal Vault

Securely store your most sensitive files in the cloud with Personal Vault in OneDrive and access them anywhere you go using identity verification.1

Overview

Help safeguard important information

  • Help protect digital copies of your passport, driver’s license, and other documents in Personal Vault. Your files will be secured by identity verification, yet easily accessible across your devices.
  • Conveniently unlock and access Personal Vault files with your fingerprint, face, or SMS code.2
  • Use the OneDrive mobile app on iOS or Android devices to scan documents, take pictures, and shoot videos directly into Personal Vault.
  • Personal Vault automatically relocks on your PC, on your device, or online after a short period of inactivity.3 Any open files also lock and require reauthentication to access.

A versatile mobile lockbox

Easily reach sensitive and important files on the go.
Available on any device
