Overview

Your favorite memories protected, accessible, and shareable

  • Protect your memories and make space for new ones by automatically backing them up to OneDrive.
  • Easily find and relive a lifetime of memories.
  • Access and edit your photos and video across all your devices, anywhere.
  • Share your favorite moments with the people who matter.
KEY FEATURES

Digitize what matters

With the mobile app, scan documents and meaningful mementos to keep them protected and accessible across devices.

Make your memories shine

Edit photos and create meaningful designs in seconds with the power of AI.

Moments of joy

OneDrive guides you back in time to relive your precious memories, whether from a week or a decade ago.
