Overview

Let Copilot handle the busywork

Boost productivity by using Microsoft Copilot with your OneDrive files.
  • Easily leverage summarize, compare, or transform your files in to audio overviews using Copilot's floating button in your OneDrive experience. With Copilot, OneDrive learns what you’re working on and brings the next step forward—no more digging through folders or switching tabs. Get answers, not just files, and move with momentum.
    Try Copilot in OneDrive
  • OneDrive is built to be there the moment you need it—fast, seamless, and ready across every device. Enjoy a modern experience on web, desktop, and mobile, with instant shortcuts, smarter syncing, and real-time collaboration. Your work is always saved and always accessible.
    Explore OneDrive features
  • Share files securely and simply with the new Hero Link—one super-powered link for all access. Copilot-powered summaries give your team instant clarity, while bulk file transfers and archiving keep projects moving. OneDrive makes teamwork seamless, secure, and smarter.
  • Back up and help protect your files with OneDrive. Easily recover files from accidental deletion or malicious attacks. And administrators can manage security policies to help keep your information safe.
