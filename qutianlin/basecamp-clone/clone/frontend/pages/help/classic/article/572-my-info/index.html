Edit personal information

To add or change your photo, click the “My info” link that appears in the upper right of every screen.

Click the link to edit your personal information, and you'll be able to change your name, avatar or photo, email address, Basecamp username, password, and time zone. (Your avatar can only be updated on the web browser, not on the mobile site or app.)

Change my notification address

You can easily specify a different address for each Basecamp account you’re a part of. Whatever email address you choose for the account will be the address Basecamp sends email notifications to.

To change this, go to the My Info page and you’ll see a field under “Your contact information for this Basecamp account."

Note: This will not change the email on file for your Basecamp username. If you forget your login and need to reset it, you need to enter the email on file for your Basecamp username.

Change the language

Basecamp Classic is available in over a dozen languages:

Chinese (simplified), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, or Swedish.

To change your language, go to your “My Info” link in the top right corner. You’ll have a language option there.
