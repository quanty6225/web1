Plans and pricing

Basecamp offers lots of different plans, so your account is always the perfect fit. We try to keep things affordable and on a level playing field for everyone – our prices are among the lowest in the industry!

10 Projects ($25/month)

  • 3 GB storage
  • Unlimited users
  • SSL encryption

40 Projects ($60/month)

  • 15 GB storage
  • Unlimited users
  • SSL encryption

100 Projects ($120/month) 

  • 40 GB storage
  • Unlimited users
  • SSL encryption

Unlimited projects ($180/month)

  • 500 GB storage
  • Unlimited users
  • SSL encryption

  1. Ready to pick a plan? You can do that from your account page. You won't make a mistake, because you're never locked into a plan. Upgrade or downgrade whenever you need to, month to month.
  2. Need more storage? You can upgrade that, without adding more projects.
  3. Are you a teacher? Learn more about Basecamp for Teachers.
  4. Are you a nonprofit? We offer a 10% nonprofit discount off the plan of your choice. We’ll just need to see some documentation (e.g. IRS 501c3 status letter) of your nonprofit status. Email us at nonprofits@basecamp.com and we’ll be glad to apply the discount.
  5. Basecamp Classic owners can use Basecamp 2 free of charge, on the equivalent plan. We'll credit your account towards that plan. For example, if you use the Plus plan in Basecamp Classic, we'll give you a $50 credit for the new Basecamp. If you use the 40 project plan, it's free for you! If you upgrade to a larger plan, we'll charge for the difference.

Learn more about migrating your Classic account.

If you cancel your Basecamp Classic account or downgrade it to the Free plan, you'll be billed for Basecamp 2.
